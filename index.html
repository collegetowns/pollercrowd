Poller Crowd

Warm up the lecture.

Live polls, word clouds, quizzes, rankings and Q&A for classes, lectures, workshops and conference talks. Your audience scans a QR code and answers from their phones. No app, no accounts, and answers stay anonymous.

Students & audiences

Joining a session?

No app or account needed. Scan the QR code on the big screen, or type the code here. Your answers stay anonymous.

Teachers & presenters

Running a class or a talk?

Build a question set, put it on the projector, and watch the answers roll in. Everything you make is saved on your own computer.

Heads up: the shared connection handles about 200 people at once, counting every room using Poller Crowd at that moment. For big audiences or regular use, connect your own free Supabase or Firebase (about 5 minutes, and an AI assistant can walk you through it) so the limit is yours alone.

What you can ask

7 question types, plus info slides for titles and breaks
  • Multiple choice

    Live bars, with an optional correct answer to reveal.

  • Word cloud

    Popular words grow big. Rude ones turn into #!*&.

  • Rating scale

    1 to 5 or 1 to 10, with an average and a chart.

  • Open Q&A

    Anonymous questions that everyone can upvote.

  • Quiz race

    A competition: faster right answers score more, with a trophy podium at the end.

  • Ranking

    Everyone puts items in order; see what the room agrees on.

  • Thermometer

    Name two poles; everyone slides to where they think it sits.

  • Info slide

    A welcome, instructions or a break, with an optional countdown.

Pick a buddy, any buddy

Ten polar buddies, four color patterns and five looks. That’s 200 combinations, so a whole room can be different.
