Privacy

What Poller Crowd keeps (almost nothing)

Poller Crowd is built so that schools don’t have to worry about student data.

Students

  • No accounts, names or emails. Students join with a room code and pick a cartoon buddy. A nickname is optional and only shows on quiz leaderboards.
  • Answers are anonymous. The teacher sees buddies, not people.
  • Nothing is stored on a server. Answers travel from the phone to the teacher’s screen through a live connection (Supabase Realtime) and are not saved there.
  • The phone remembers a random ID, its buddy and its own answers so a page reload doesn’t lose them. These are deleted after a week.

Teachers

  • Question sets and results are saved only in the teacher’s own browser. They never leave that computer unless the teacher exports them.
  • Past sessions are deleted automatically after 30 days. Teachers can delete them sooner in Settings.

Visitor counts

We may count page visits with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and doesn’t track people across sites. Only the page name is counted (like “join.html”), never room codes, answers or anything typed.

Fonts and code

Fonts and code libraries are served from this site, so your browser doesn’t contact Google Fonts or other outside services to show the page.

Rude words

Common swear words and slurs are replaced with symbols like #!*& before they reach the big screen. No filter catches everything, so teachers can also hold word cloud and Q&A answers for approval, hide anything, and remove a student from the room.

Questions

Use the and we’ll get back to you. If your school needs a formal data agreement, the short version is: no student personal data is collected or stored.
